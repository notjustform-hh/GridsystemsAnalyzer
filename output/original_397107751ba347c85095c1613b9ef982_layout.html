Letterform Construction: N

The letter "N" demonstrates fundamental principles of sans-serif construction. Two vertical stems connected by a diagonal stroke create structural stability while maintaining optical balance. The diagonal element provides dynamic tension within the otherwise static vertical framework.

In typography, the "N" serves as a benchmark for establishing stroke width, stem thickness, and overall proportional relationships that define a typeface's character.

Letterform Construction: U

The letter "U" showcases the integration of linear and curved elements in typographic construction. The two vertical stems transition into a curved base, creating a harmonious balance between geometric precision and organic flow.

This letterform exemplifies how typography must reconcile structural requirements with aesthetic refinement, demonstrating the architectural principles underlying all letterform design.
