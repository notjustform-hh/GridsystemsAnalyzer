## 9. Responsive Adaptation Notes
The organic poster design with integrated typography and imagery will face challenges on mobile devices. The large "Toro Y Moi" title that creates strong poster impact will need significant scaling, potentially losing its dominant visual presence. The piano keyboard graphic that provides musical connection may become too small to read clearly on mobile screens. The background organic photography with overlaid curved line elements will lose detail and atmospheric quality when compressed. Most critically, the tour dates table with four columns (date, location, venue, status) will require restructuring - likely stacking venue information vertically rather than the current horizontal tabular format. The organic curved line elements that create visual flow across the poster may need to be simplified or removed entirely as they become ineffective at small sizes. The artistic integration between typography, keyboard graphic, and background imagery that defines the poster's sophisticated aesthetic will be reduced to sequential content blocks. Consider creating a mobile version that prioritizes the artist name and essential tour information while maintaining the musical keyboard element as a simplified header graphic. The organic background could be used as a subtle mobile background pattern rather than the dominant atmospheric element.
