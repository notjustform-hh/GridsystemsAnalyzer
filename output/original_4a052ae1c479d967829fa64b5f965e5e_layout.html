Biography
Willi Kunz, pioneering graphic designer in New York, has been creating responsible design solutions and educating designers about design principles for over four decades. His work demonstrates a commitment to clarity, functionality, and systematic thinking.
Design Philosophy
The Swiss-born designer believes typography forms the foundation of all visual communication. His approach emphasizes grid systems, typography hierarchies, and systematic design thinking as essential tools for effective visual problem solving.
Teaching & Legacy
Through his teaching and practice, Kunz has influenced generations of designers. His work encompasses cultural institutions, corporate identity, and educational projects, always maintaining the highest standards of typographic excellence and systematic design approach.
