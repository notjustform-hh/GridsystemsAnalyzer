# Layout Specification: Apse Magazine Double-Page Spread

## 1. Format & Proportions
- **Orientation**: Landscape spread
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1.56:1 (overall spread)
- **Page Ratio vs φ**: 1.56 (φ = 1.618) ✓ 96% proximity to golden ratio
- **Assumed Format**: Magazine spread with spiral binding, likely 11×8.5" or similar editorial format
- **Spread or Single**: Double-page spread with spiral binding

## 2. Margins
- **Top**: 30px (3.3%)
- **Right/Outer**: 30px (2.2% per page)
- **Bottom**: 30px (3.3%)
- **Left/Inner**: 30px (2.2% per page)
- **Gutter**: 60px (4.3%) spiral binding area
- **Symmetry**: Symmetrical margins with spiral binding accommodation
- **Notes**: Consistent 30px margins with wider spiral binding area affecting reading flow

## 3. Grid System
- **Grid Type**: Asymmetric editorial layout with typography emphasis
- **Column Count per Page**: Left: 4 columns below main type | Right: 4 columns for text/image
- **Total Columns (spread)**: 8 columns with different content strategies
- **Column Widths**: Left: 4×120px text columns | Right: mixed 160px-180px columns
- **Column Width (px)**: Left page: 120px × 4 | Right: 180px, 180px, 120px mixed
- **Gutter Width**: 20px between columns, 60px spiral binding
- **Row/Zone Structure**: Main typography(400px) | Text area(220px) | Lower content(140px)
- **Baseline Grid**: Editorial freedom approach
- **Compound Grid**: Yes - large typography overlay with columnar text system
- **Confidence**: High
- **Reasoning**: Clear asymmetric division with large typography dominating left page, systematic text grid on right

## 5. Visual Hierarchy
1. **Primary**: Large "apse" typography - dominates 35% of spread area
2. **Secondary**: Architectural photographs - documentary evidence supporting theme
3. **Tertiary**: Editorial text columns - supporting informational content
4. **Reading flow**: "apse" typography → Right main text → Architectural images → Supporting text

## 6. Spacing & Proportional System
- **Dominant spacing unit**: 20px (column gutters) and 30px (margins)
- **Golden ratio presence**: Strong - spread ratio approaches φ, typography zone positioned at golden section
- **Vertical rhythm**: Large typography zone (400px) + text zone (220px) + lower content (140px)
- **Density**: Asymmetric - dense typography left vs distributed editorial content right
- **White space strategy**: Spiral binding creates reading pause, typography demands spatial breathing room

## 7. Typography Observations
- **Heading scale**: Ultra-large "apse" display creates architectural scale relationship
- **Body text columns**: 4-column system provides readable measure for editorial content
- **Type scale**: Extreme contrast - massive display vs standard editorial text
- **Alignment**: Left-aligned editorial text, centered display typography
- **Special typography**: "apse" connects typographically to architectural content theme

## 8. Design Principles Detected
- **Typography as architecture**: "apse" word choice and scale mirrors architectural spaces
- **Editorial magazine format**: Professional layout balancing visual impact with information
- **Asymmetric emphasis**: Left page impact balanced by right page editorial function
- **Spiral binding integration**: Binding method affects reading flow and page relationship
- **Content-form relationship**: Typography choice reinforces architectural subject matter
- **Scale as meaning**: Large typography suggests architectural scale and spatial experience

## 9. Responsive Adaptation Notes
The dramatic left-page typography dominance will be severely compromised on mobile, losing the architectural scale effect that connects form to content. The large "apse" typography that currently occupies 35% of spread area will be compressed, diminishing its spatial relationship to the architectural theme. The 4-column editorial grid will stack vertically, eliminating the sophisticated text/image integration that supports the main typography. The spiral binding context will be lost, removing an important tactile quality that affects the reading experience. Most critically, the asymmetric balance between typographic impact and editorial content will collapse to linear flow, fundamentally altering the magazine's editorial rhythm. Consider maintaining the large "apse" typography as a full-screen mobile element to preserve some architectural impact, while accepting that the sophisticated editorial layout integration will be simplified.
