# Layout Specification: Fashion Editorial - Geometric Shape & Model Double-Page Spread

## 1. Format & Proportions
- **Orientation**: Landscape spread
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1.55:1
- **Page Ratio vs φ**: 1.55 (φ = 1.618) ✓ 96% proximity to golden ratio
- **Assumed Format**: Fashion magazine, likely large format editorial (A3 or custom)
- **Spread or Single**: Double-page spread with minimal central spine

## 2. Margins
- **Top**: 20px (1.8%)
- **Right/Outer**: 20px (1.2% per page)
- **Bottom**: 20px (1.8%)
- **Left/Inner**: 20px (1.2% per page)
- **Gutter**: 50px (2.9%) minimal central spine
- **Symmetry**: Minimal symmetrical margins maximizing image area
- **Notes**: Tight 20px margins create maximum visual impact for editorial content

## 3. Grid System
- **Grid Type**: Asymmetric editorial layout with geometric emphasis
- **Column Count per Page**: Left: single geometric zone | Right: photography + text zones
- **Total Columns (spread)**: 3 major zones (geometric shape, model photo, editorial text)
- **Column Widths**: Left: 775px (45.6%) | Right: 775px split (photography 450px, text 120px)
- **Column Width (px)**: Left geometric: 775px | Right photo: 450px | Right text: 120px
- **Gutter Width**: 50px central spine, 20px between photo and text on right
- **Row/Zone Structure**: Header(30px) | Main content(940px) | Footer(30px)
- **Baseline Grid**: Minimal - focused on visual impact over text structure
- **Compound Grid**: Yes - geometric abstraction overlay with photography grid
- **Confidence**: High
- **Reasoning**: Clear asymmetric division with dramatic geometric shape dominating left, photography and editorial text on right

## 5. Visual Hierarchy
1. **Primary**: Large black geometric shape - dominates 26% of spread width and 55% of height
2. **Secondary**: Fashion model photography - strong visual counterpoint to geometric abstraction
3. **Tertiary**: Vertical "VIBERLMAISON BOOK" typography - brand integration
4. **Quaternary**: Editorial credits and production information - supporting details
5. **Reading flow**: Geometric shape → Model photograph → Vertical brand text → Editorial credits

## 6. Spacing & Proportional System
- **Dominant spacing unit**: 20px (margins) and 50px (central spine)
- **Golden ratio presence**: Very strong - spread ratio 1.55 very close to φ (1.618)
- **Vertical rhythm**: Minimal structure prioritizing visual impact over text rhythm
- **Density**: Asymmetric - dense geometric abstraction left vs integrated photography/text right
- **White space strategy**: Minimal margins maximize visual impact, white space used for typography placement

## 7. Typography Observations
- **Heading scale**: Small headers maintain focus on visual content
- **Brand typography**: Vertical "VIBERLMAISON BOOK" creates strong graphic element
- **Editorial text**: Minimal text optimized for production credits and model information
- **Type integration**: Typography serves photography and geometric composition
- **Alignment**: Vertical text creates dynamic relationship with geometric shape
- **Special typography**: Vertical orientation reinforces editorial magazine sophistication

## 8. Design Principles Detected
- **Abstract-figurative contrast**: Geometric minimalism opposed to fashion photography
- **Asymmetric balance**: Large black shape balanced by detailed photography and text
- **Editorial sophistication**: Fashion magazine layout with architectural design approach
- **Visual dialogue**: Form (geometric) and content (fashion) create conceptual tension
- **Minimalist aesthetic**: Reduction to essential elements maximizes visual impact
- **Brand integration**: Vertical typography maintains commercial fashion context

## 9. Responsive Adaptation Notes
The dramatic asymmetric balance between abstract geometric form and fashion photography will be severely compromised on mobile devices. The large black geometric shape that currently occupies 26% of spread width will lose its spatial relationship to the model photograph, breaking the abstract-figurative dialogue that defines the editorial concept. The vertical "VIBERLMAISON BOOK" typography will become horizontal, losing the sophisticated vertical integration that reinforces the magazine's editorial approach. The model photography will stack below the geometric shape, eliminating the simultaneous visual comparison that creates conceptual tension. Most critically, the asymmetric balance that creates fashion editorial sophistication will collapse to linear content flow, fundamentally altering the magazine's design approach. The minimal text structure will remain readable, but the architectural design relationship between form and photography will be lost. Consider maintaining the geometric shape as a background element to preserve some visual relationship, while accepting that the sophisticated spatial dialogue will be simplified to sequential content presentation.
